# Corpus

A BLANK form exactly as its administration publishes it. Never a document issued to somebody:
an issued document cannot be fully redacted (a 2D barcode encoding the identity, an intact text
layer under the black rectangle, XMP metadata, incremental update history, scanner fingerprint).

The Etalab Open Licence 2.0 on the Cerfa makes naming the PRODUCER and the LAST UPDATE DATE
mandatory wherever the file is redistributed. Both are in the table below, and they must travel
with the file.

THREE LANGUAGES, AND THE THIRD ONE IS THERE TO BREAK A CONFOUND. The corpus was two English
forms plus one French one, and the measurement said something uncomfortable: all 4 of the false
alarms on clean dossiers, out of 864, land on the French form. But "French" and "Cerfa layout"
were the same thing in that corpus, so nothing could tell whether the tool struggles with the
LANGUAGE or with fields boxed character by character. fw9sp.pdf is the IRS's own Spanish W-9:
same producer, same licence, same 23 declared zones, and a comb tax number with byte-identical
geometry to the English one. It varies the language while holding the layout, which is the only
way to separate the two.
